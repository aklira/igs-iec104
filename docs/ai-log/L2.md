# L2 — State machine core

## Prompt (summarised)

Task L2 of IMPLEMENTATION.md: `Session`, sans I/O, with `handle(event, now)`
returning actions. Events: bytes received, tick, user sends an ASDU, user
starts or stops the data transfer. Actions: send an APDU, deliver an ASDU,
schedule a wake-up, close with a reason. Sequence numbers modulo 32768, send
window k, acknowledgement after w received I frames, STARTDT and STOPDT states.
Done when deterministic tests with a fake clock cover: normal exchange, window
full, wrap-around at 32768, and a received N(R) that acknowledges frames never
sent, as §5.1 specifies (or a question recorded when the text is silent).

(STARTDT and STOPDT), `007_5.2_*` (test procedure, for the TESTFR rule), and

## Summary

`crates/igs-iec104-link/src/session.rs`:

- `Session::new(config)`: a connection just established. The sequence numbers
  are zero and the transfer is `Stopped`.
- `handle(event, now) -> Vec<Action>`:
  - `Event::Received(bytes)`: any number of frames, possibly split over events,
    decoded by the codec's `FrameDecoder`.
  - `Event::SendAsdu(asdu)`: an I frame, or `Rejected(TransferStopped)`, or
    `Rejected(WindowFull)`.
  - `Event::StartDt` and `Event::StopDt`: the controlling station starts and
    stops; the controlled station gets `Rejected(NotForRole)`.
  - `Event::Tick`: nothing yet (timers are L3).
- Actions: `Send(Apdu)`, `Deliver(Asdu)`, `Rejected(Rejection)`,
  `Close(CloseReason)`. A closed session ignores every event.
- `TransferState`: `Stopped`, `PendingStarted` and `Started` for both roles;
  `PendingStopped` and `PendingUnconfirmedStop` for the stop. The controlled
  station never enters `PendingStarted` or `PendingStopped`.

Rules implemented, all roles:

- V(S) and V(R) are 15-bit numbers. Distances are taken modulo 32768 with
  `wrapping_sub` and a mask, so the wrap needs no special case.
- An I frame is sent with N(R) = V(R), the piggybacked acknowledgement. An S
  frame is sent when w received frames are not yet acknowledged (§5.5).
- A send is refused when k frames are unacknowledged (`WindowFull`).
- An N(R) is valid when it lies between the last acknowledged number and V(S),
  inclusive. Otherwise the connection closes (Q-003).
- An I frame whose N(S) is not V(R) closes the connection (Q-005).
- A TESTFR act is confirmed in every state (§5.2).

Controlled station (figure 17):

- Stopped: STARTDT act gives STARTDT con and `Started`. I and S frames close the
  connection. STOPDT act in Stopped has no action (Q-007).
- Started: STOPDT act confirms the received frames with an S frame first (§5.3).
  Then STOPDT con and `Stopped` if no sent frame is unacknowledged, otherwise
  `PendingUnconfirmedStop`.
- PendingUnconfirmedStop: an I frame closes the connection. An S frame that
  acknowledges everything gives STOPDT con and `Stopped`.

Controlling station (figure 18):

- Stopped: `StartDt` gives STARTDT act and `PendingStarted`. I and S frames
  close the connection.
- PendingStarted: STARTDT con gives `Started`. I and S frames close the
  connection.
- Started: `StopDt` first confirms the received frames with an S frame (§5.3),
  then sends STOPDT act and moves to `PendingStopped` if nothing is
  unacknowledged, or to `PendingUnconfirmedStop` otherwise. `StartDt` is refused
  with `InvalidState`.
- PendingUnconfirmedStop: an I or S frame that acknowledges everything moves to
  `PendingStopped`. I frames are accepted and answered at once with an S frame
  (Q-008). STOPDT con gives `Stopped`.
- PendingStopped: I frames are accepted and answered at once with an S frame
  (Q-008); S frames are accepted. STOPDT con gives `Stopped`.
- Sends are refused in every state except `Started` (`TransferStopped`).

Verification: `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace
--all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace
--all-features` (158 tests; 54 in the link crate: 10 for the parameters, 44 for
the session) and `cargo deny check` are all green.

Tests, all with an explicit fake clock:

- normal exchange, with an acknowledgement after every eighth frame;
- the window full at k = 12, refusal, and the window opening again;
- wrap-around at 32768 in both directions (received and sent numbers), over
  more than 32 768 frames each;
- a window that crosses the wrap;
- an acknowledgement of frames never sent, and the boundary value that is
  valid;
- an unexpected N(S);
- the controlled station's start and stop, with and without unconfirmed frames;
- the controlling station's start and stop, with and without unconfirmed frames,
  the pending states, and the refusals;
- frames split at every byte boundary, and several frames in one chunk;
- framing and decoding errors, and the ignoring of events after a close.

## Decisions

- The close reasons for N(R), N(S) and decoding are open questions (Q-003,
  Q-005, Q-006), not decisions. The code closes the connection, the conservative
  choice.
- Where the text and figure 18 differ, the text is followed (Q-008): the
  controlling station answers an I frame at once while the stop is pending, and
  confirms the received frames before its STOPDT act.
- The session returns the ASDU of a refused send to the user, and never queues
  it.
