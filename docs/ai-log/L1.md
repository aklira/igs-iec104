# L1 — Parameters

## Prompt (summarised)

Task L1 of IMPLEMENTATION.md: `LinkConfig` with t0, t1, t2, t3, k and w. The
defaults come from IEC 60870-5-104 §9.6, and the ranges and relations that the
standard states are validated. The configuration also records the role of the
station (controlling or controlled). Done when tests cover the defaults and
invalid configurations.

Inputs read: `104/sections/040_9.6_*` (the time-outs and window table, and the
basic application functions) and `104/sections/010_5.5_*` (the maximum number of
outstanding I frames, k, and the acknowledgement window, w).

## Summary

- `crates/igs-iec104-link/src/config.rs`:
  - `Role`: `Controlling` or `Controlled`.
  - `Parameters`: plain values (t0, t1, t2, t3 as `Duration`, k and w as `u16`).
    `Default` gives the §9.6 values: t0 30 s, t1 15 s, t2 10 s, t3 20 s, k 12, w 8.
  - `LinkConfig::new(role, parameters)`: the only way to get a validated
    configuration. `LinkConfig::with_defaults(role)` gives the defaults.
  - `LinkConfig::recommendations()`: the values the standard recommends against,
    without rejecting them.
  - `ConfigError`: `NotWholeSeconds`, `TimeoutOutOfRange`, `T2NotBelowT1`,
    `WindowOutOfRange`. `Recommendation`: `WindowAboveTwoThirdsOfK`, `LongT3`.
- `crates/igs-iec104-link/src/lib.rs`: wires `pub mod config;` and re-exports the
  types.

Checks applied by `LinkConfig::new`:

- t0, t1 and t2: whole seconds, from 1 to 255.
- t3: whole seconds, at least 1 (no upper bound here; see below).
- t2 strictly below t1.
- k and w: from 1 to 32767.

Recommendations reported by `recommendations()`:

- w above two thirds of k, tested as 3w > 2k so that no division is needed.
- t3 above 48 hours.

Verification: `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace
--all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace
--all-features` (114 tests, of which 10 are in the link crate) and `cargo deny
check` are all green.

Tests:

- The defaults are the values of the standard, and the default configuration is
  valid for both roles, with no recommendation.
- Boundary values are accepted: 1 s and 255 s, t2 at 254 s below t1 at 255 s,
  k and w at 1 and at 32767.
- Each timeout out of range is rejected (zero or 256 s), including t3 at zero.
- A fraction of a second is rejected for t1 and for t3.
- t2 equal to t1, or above it, is rejected.
- k and w at 0, 32768 and 65535 are rejected.
- w = 9 with k = 12 gives the window recommendation, w = 8 does not. A t3 of 48
  hours and one second gives the long-t3 recommendation only above 48 hours.
- Error messages name the parameter.

## Decisions

- D-012 (PROVENANCE.md): the standard's maximum ranges are errors, its
  recommendations are warnings. The lower bound of t3 (1 s) is an error. The
  upper bound of t3 is a recommendation, so a configuration with t3 of 49 hours is
  accepted with a warning.
- Timeouts are `std::time::Duration`. They are plain values of a sans-I/O
  configuration, not clocks.
- The role is stored with the parameters. Nothing in L1 changes behaviour with
  the role: the state machine (L2) will use it.
- `Parameters` has public fields because it is only data; `LinkConfig` has
  private fields, because its invariants (t2 below t1, the ranges) can only be
  checked together.

## Points for the reviewer

- Only the defaults and the ranges are in L1. The timer behaviour (what happens
  when t1, t2 or t3 expires) belongs to L3.
- The check that w is at most k is not made: the standard states no such
  requirement, only the two-thirds recommendation. A maintainer may want to make
  w above k an error; D-012 would then need a new entry.
- Size: `config.rs` is about 220 lines of code and about 220 lines of tests.
