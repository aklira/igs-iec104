# U1 — Graceful server shutdown

## Prompt (summarised)

Task U1 of IMPLEMENTATION.md (phase 6): a way to stop a running `Server`: it stops accepting
connections, closes its connections within a deadline given by the caller, and returns. The
maintainer validated the recommended API: `Server::run_until(stop, grace) -> ShutdownReport`, with
`run` kept, and the events still queued at the stop lost and counted. The maintainer asked for it
with "commence U1".

## Status

**Done in the working tree.** The box for U1 in IMPLEMENTATION.md §6 is ticked in the commit that
adds it.

## Summary

- **API.** `Server::run_until(self, stop: impl Future<Output = ()>, grace: Duration) ->
  ShutdownReport`. `ShutdownReport { connections, forced, pending_events }`. `run` is
  `run_until(pending, 0)`.
- **Order of the stop.** When `stop` completes, the listener is dropped. A `watch` signal of the
  station tells every connection to stop: it hands the ASDUs it has already taken to the transport,
  takes no new event, and closes its command channel, so the transport sends them and shuts the
  stream down. Its leftover events go to the carry queue of the group, as at any end of connection
  (D-021 applies). A connection in its TLS handshake is not served. The station sends no STOPDT:
  that request belongs to the controlling station.
- **Grace and force.** The connections are tasks of a `JoinSet`. After `grace`, those still open
  are aborted. A connection's transport runs in its own task; a guard aborts it when the connection
  is dropped, so a forced close closes the stream too.
- **Report.** `pending_events` is the carry queue of the group plus the events of the image no
  connection took: events not sent, or sent and not acknowledged.
- **Change of behaviour.** The connections used to be detached tasks: dropping the `run` future left
  them serving. They are now owned by `run_until`, so dropping it closes them. Recorded under
  "Changed" in the CHANGELOG.
- **Binary.** `igs104-server --for` stops with `run_until` and a grace of one second.
- **Tests.** `tests/server.rs`: a stop without connection returns at once and refuses new
  connections; a stop closes an open connection within the grace period (the client sees
  `Disconnected`); the events no connection took are counted. `src/server/tests.rs`: the forced
  close of a connection that never ends, no force for connections that close in time (paused
  clock), and the abort of a transport with its connection. A forced close over TCP was not tested
  end to end: with a zero grace period, the connection closes on its own before tokio's timer fires,
  and a connection that cannot close in order cannot be made reliably on loopback.
