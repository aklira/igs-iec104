# S3 — Redundancy groups

## Prompt (summarised)

Task S3 of IMPLEMENTATION.md: groups of connections where only the connection that received STARTDT
carries data; switchover on STARTDT on another connection; retransmission of unacknowledged I frames
after switchover as §10.5 and §10.6 specify; client-side redundancy for the controlling station. Done
when fake-clock tests cover switchover with and without in-flight frames, with no data loss. Depends on
S2. The plan says the state diagrams of §10 are missing and asks for the PDF figures through QUESTIONS.md
before implementing. The figures are in the standard's PDF (English pages 129 to 147), so they were read
from it (Q-016), and the work went ahead. The maintainer asked for it with "commence S3"; the work stays in
the working tree until a commit is requested.

## Figures read from the PDF

The prepared text has the clauses of §10 but not figures 32 to 37. They were read from the rendered pages
(English pages 129, 133, 137, 141, 145, 147). The transitions below are written in our words; no text of
the figures is copied.

- **Figure 32 (controlling station, initialization).** After the connections are established, the
  controlling station starts one of them (STARTDT act). The station interrogation follows the start.
  The other connections stay stopped, and are supervised by TESTFR.
- **Figure 33 (controlled station, initialization).** After the STARTDT con, the controlled station sends
  M_EI on the started connection. The application's ENDINIT request is optional. This settles Q-014.
- **Figure 34 (user data from the controlling station).** A command is lost on a connection that fails.
  After t1 the application starts another connection. The controlled side closes the old one when the
  new STARTDT act arrives, and the application repeats the command on the new connection.
- **Figure 35 (user data from the controlled station).** An event is lost on a failed connection. After
  the switch, the controlled station sends the event again on the new started connection, after the
  STARTDT con.
- **Figure 36 (controlled station, state diagram).** STOPPED: a STARTDT act closes the other connections
  that are not stopped, and confirms (→ STARTED). STARTED: a STOPDT act without unacknowledged I frames is
  confirmed at once (→ STOPPED); with them, it waits (→ pending unconfirmed stop). A t1 timeout, or the
  close by another connection, closes it. Pending unconfirmed stop: an S frame with no unacknowledged I
  frame sends the STOPDT con; t1 or an I frame closes it. No figure arrow closes it on another connection
  starting (Q-017).
- **Figure 37 (controlling station, state diagram).** STOPPED: a start sends STARTDT act (→ pending
  started). Pending started: the STARTDT con starts it (→ STARTED); t1, an S or I frame, or a request to
  change the connection, closes it. STARTED: a stop sends STOPDT act (→ pending stopped, or pending
  unconfirmed stop); a t1 timeout or a request to change the connection closes it. Pending stopped: the
  STOPDT con (→ STOPPED); t1 closes it.

## Summary

- `crates/igs-iec104-link/src/session.rs`: the session keeps the ASDUs of its unacknowledged I frames
  (`unacknowledged()`), and takes `Event::Supersede`. A superseded connection closes with
  `CloseReason::Superseded` when its transfer is not stopped (figures 36 and 37; Q-017 for the pending
  stop of a controlled station).
- `crates/igs-iec104/src/transport.rs`: `Command::Supersede`; `Delivery::Unacknowledged(Vec<Asdu>)`, which
  every connection hands back when it ends, whatever ended it.
- `crates/igs-iec104/src/server.rs`: the server is now a redundancy group (§10.2). All its connections
  share one process image, and only one is started at a time. Starting a connection supersedes the others
  that are not stopped, which the group does by notifying them. The image is the event buffer: only the
  started connection takes its events. The ASDUs of a superseded connection that the peer did not
  acknowledge, those refused by the window, and those not yet sent are kept in order in the group's carry
  queue, and the next started connection sends them first (§10.6). A connection that is started and
  superseded hands its leftovers to the carry queue, and the started connection is woken to take them.
- `crates/igs-iec104/src/redundancy.rs`: the controlling station with redundant connections,
  `RedundantClient`. One T2 `Client` per endpoint, each with its own reconnection. The first endpoint that
  connects is started, and gets a station interrogation (§10.3, figure 32). When the started one is lost,
  the next connected endpoint is started, with a station interrogation (§10.5). `switch_to` is a manual
  switchover: STOPDT on the started connection, then STARTDT on the chosen one (§10.2). The unacknowledged
  ASDUs of a lost connection reach the application as `Delivery::Unacknowledged`; the application decides
  whether to send them again (§10.5 leaves commands to the application).
- Tests: 5 switchover tests of the server with a fake clock (paused time, long link timers, in-memory
  streams); 4 tests of the redundancy group over loopback with relays that can be cut; the link tests of
  the kept ASDUs and of the supersede event (5 new).

## Defects found and fixed while testing

- **Refused ASDUs were sent in reverse order.** When the window is full, the transport refuses each ASDU in
  turn; the first version put each refused ASDU at the front of the queue, so the queue came out reversed.
  The window test (30 events over a window of 12) caught it. Checked by reversing the order again: the test
  then fails with the events out of order, and passes once restored.
- **A livelock on a full window.** A refused ASDU was sent again at once, and refused again, with no time
  passing. It is now sent again on the retry timer only.
- **The first interrogation was missing from the controlling station.** §10.3 and figure 32 require the
  station interrogation after the first start too, not only after a switchover. Corrected.

## Decisions

- **One server is one redundancy group.** Several groups, which rule 7 of §10.2 requires for several
  controlling stations, are several servers on several ports. A routing table by peer address was not
  added.
- **The image is the event buffer.** Events raised while no connection is started wait in the queue (up to
  its capacity, then the oldest is dropped and counted). No event is lost at a switchover.
- **Only the started connection takes events.** The broadcast of S2 is removed. A second start supersedes
  the first, so the S2 test that expected two started connections to both receive the events now expects
  the supersede rule (the second connection takes the events). The S2 log describes the earlier dispatcher.
- **Retransmission uses the link's acknowledgements.** A frame is unacknowledged until the peer's N(R)
  covers it. A frame the peer received, but whose acknowledgement had not yet been sent, is sent again:
  the duplicate is possible under §10.6. Q-018 asked whether "confirmed" means the link acknowledgement; D-014 decides it.
- **Supersede by the text where the figure is silent.** A controlled connection whose stop waits for its
  acknowledgements is closed when another connection starts (§10.7), although figure 36 has no arrow for
  it. Q-017.
- **Client interrogation.** A station interrogation follows every start: the first (figure 32, §10.3), and
  each switchover (§10.5), so that no change is missed.
- **Commands are not retransmitted by the group.** §10.5 leaves that to the application. The group hands
  the unacknowledged ASDUs to the application as `Delivery::Unacknowledged`.
- **Refused ASDUs are retried on the timer** (10 ms), and never at once.

## Verification

- `cargo test -p igs-iec104-link`: all pass (5 new tests for the kept ASDUs and the supersede event).
- `cargo test -p igs-iec104 --lib server::tests`: 5 pass, with paused time. The window test was
  mutation-checked (see above).
- `cargo test -p igs-iec104 --test server`: 17 pass (the S2 test about two started connections changed to
  the supersede rule).
- `cargo test -p igs-iec104 --lib redundancy`: 4 pass, over loopback in real time, about 0.2 s.
- `cargo test --workspace --all-features`: 278 tests passed, none failed.
- `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`,
  `cargo deny check`, `cargo doc`: clean.

## Not covered by tests

- A controlling station that receives unacknowledged ASDUs from a lost connection and sends them again on
  the new one: the group hands them to the application, but no test checks the application's
  retransmission.
- Two groups on the server: the rule that several controlling stations use different groups is the
  deployment's (several servers), not tested.
- The bench: no interop scenario exercises the switchover against the reference peers. The reference
  server of the T4 bench is not configured for redundancy.
