## Summary

- `crates/igs-iec104-link/src/session/timers.rs` (new): the three deadlines and
  their rules.
- `Session::new(config, now)`: takes the instant the connection is established,
  so t3 can start. Callers and tests pass it.
- `Session::next_deadline()`: the earliest running deadline, or `None`. The
  caller calls `handle(Event::Tick)` at or after it.
- `handle`: runs the timers due at `now` first, then the event, then stops t1 if
  nothing awaits a confirmation.
- `CloseReason::T1Expired`: the reason for a t1 expiry.

Rules implemented:

- t1 (time-out of send or test APDUs): restarted by every I frame sent, and by
  every start act, stop act and test act sent. It stops once every sent I frame
  is acknowledged and no start, stop or test act is pending. A partial
  acknowledgement does not restart it. Its expiry closes the connection, in
  every state.
- t2 (time-out for acknowledgements without data): starts at the first received
  I frame not yet acknowledged. Later frames do not restart it. Any frame sent
  with N(R) = V(R) cancels it. Its expiry sends an S frame if a received frame is
  still unacknowledged.
- t3 (time-out for test frames in a long idle state): restarted by every frame
  received, I, S or U (§5.2). Its expiry sends a TESTFR act and starts the
  wait for its confirmation.
- TESTFR con clears the wait for a test act (§5.2). A TESTFR act is confirmed in
  every state, as before.

## Decisions

- `next_deadline()` is a query, not an action. The plan of L2 listed "schedule a
  wake-up" among the actions. A wake-up action would be repeated with every
  event and would change every action list that the tests compare. The query
  keeps the actions to what happened.
- Each `handle` call runs the due timers before the event. A tick that comes
  late therefore never loses an expiry, and a tick that comes early does
  nothing.
- A deadline that the clock cannot hold (a t3 of `u64::MAX` seconds) is `None`,
  and the timer never expires. `checked_add` is used, so nothing panics.
- t1 follows the last frame sent (figure 12 is missing: Q-009). The text names
  the timer "time-out of send or test APDUs", and figure 12 is titled "time-out
  in case of a not acknowledged last I format APDU".
- A test act is not repeated while one is unconfirmed. §5.2 does not say this;
  a second act would add nothing to the first one's wait (Q-011, Q-012 cover the
  related silences).
- Timers run in every state, Stopped included. §5.2 tests "unused, but open"
  connections, and figure 17 closes on a timeout in Stopped (Q-013).

## Verification

`cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features
-- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features` (175 tests: 71 in the
link crate, of which 10 are for the parameters, 43 for the session and 18 for the
timers) and `cargo deny check` are all green.

Timer tests, all with an explicit fake clock:

- t1: expiry for an I frame, for a start act, for a stop act and for a test act;
  restart by each I frame; no expiry once everything is acknowledged; a partial
  acknowledgement keeps the timer from the last frame; a frame that arrives after
  the expiry is not handled; expiry from the Stopped state.
- t2: the acknowledgement is sent at its deadline; later frames do not restart
  it; a sent I frame carrying the number cancels it.
- t3: a test act after an idle period, in Stopped and in Started; restart by a
  received S frame and by a received test act; one test at a time; the
  confirmation ends the wait; an unsolicited confirmation changes nothing; a t3
  that the clock cannot hold never expires.
